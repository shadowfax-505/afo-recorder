# Recorder simulation bundle

Project lead: Muttakin Rahman

The diagram, application integration and behavioral models are project-specific work. Wokwi provides the simulator and custom-chip runtime. No Wokwi simulator executable is redistributed here.

The merged firmware contains ESP-IDF v5.4.2 and its component dependencies. Their original notices and licence texts are retained under `licenses/esp-idf/` in the bundle. Project credit does not replace those notices or assert authorship of the framework and libraries.

The drawing names commercially available components for reference. It does not reproduce supplier photographs, commercial module CAD, or manufacturer PCB layouts.

The merged image is instrumented for simulation only. It must not be flashed onto the physical recorder. No physical validation is implied by the bundle.
